  S    not stated; their running tensions (337 / 113 kN, text) need e^(mu theta) = 2.98: one pulley with mu = 0.3 and ~210 deg wrap
  LL   start grip (checked, not stated as the design basis): peak tension ratio 4.25 against e^(mu alpha) = 4.81 with motor steps and no speed control
  WR   not stated
  Pa   not stated (1988 Harrison design); T_t matches about 2 % carry sag at the tail, at the foot of the 9 deg incline (phase 5.4)
  Su   holdback on the head pulley: design 1 (23.6 kN = max of grip from the installed power and 2 % sag at the tail) could transmit 20.1 of the 35.3 kNm runback torque; design 2 raised T2 to 100 kN (83.2 kNm)
